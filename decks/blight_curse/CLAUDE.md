# Blight Curse — Auntie Ool, Cursewretch

## Bracket: 3 (targeting; currently runs 0 Game Changers)

## Commander
Auntie Ool, Cursewretch {1}{B}{R}{G}
- Ward—Blight 2 (opponent puts two -1/-1 counters on a creature they control to target her)
- Whenever -1/-1 counters are put on a creature you control, draw a card
- Whenever -1/-1 counters are put on a creature you don't control, its controller loses 1 life

## Strategy
-1/-1 counter synergy deck. Spread -1/-1 counters across the board to drain opponents and draw cards. Win through incremental value, token generation (Hapatra, Flourishing Defenses, Nest of Scarabs), and attrition.

## Origin
Precon: Enduring Cursecraft (ECC), upgraded

## Key Synergies
- Counters on own creatures = card draw (Auntie Ool)
- Counters on opponents' creatures = life drain (Auntie Ool)
- Hapatra + Flourishing Defenses + Nest of Scarabs = tokens from -1/-1 counters
- Blowfly Infestation + Nest of Scarabs + Grave Venerations = infinite combo kill (drain table)
- Necroskitter / The Reaper, King No More = steal creatures that die with -1/-1 counters
- Obelisk Spider = drain on every counter placement
- Generous Patron = draw when putting counters on opponents' creatures
- Nesting Grounds = move -1/-1 counters from own creatures to opponents' (sorcery speed)
- 6 proliferate sources to multiply counters and advance sagas
- Everlasting Torment = all damage becomes -1/-1 counters (wither for everything)

## Bracket 3 Restrictions
Moved from Bracket 2 -> 3 for power-level headroom. Bracket 3 allows up to 3 Game Changers
and no early-game (first ~6 turns) two-card infinite combos; mass land denial still banned.
- Game Changers: currently 0. We have headroom for up to 3, but hold off until we find
  on-theme ones worth running (synergy over raw power). Flag any GC add explicitly.
- No Quillspike — Quillspike + Devoted Druid is an early two-card infinite (banned at Bracket 3 too).
- Blowfly + Nest of Scarabs + Grave Venerations is a THREE-card combo — fine at Bracket 3
  (only early 2-card infinites are restricted).
- Land destruction capped at TWO targeted pieces (Sundering Eruption MDFC, Demolition Field),
  both riding land slots. Deliberate flex interaction — do NOT add more LD or it tips into
  mass land denial (still banned at Bracket 3).
