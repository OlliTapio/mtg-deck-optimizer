## Counter engine + movement upgrade (Oct 2026)
More counter generation, counter movement, cheaper interaction. All cuts owned (bench).
1x Putrefy → Tear Asunder (buy) — instant, {1}{G} exiles artifact/enchantment, kicked
   {1}{G}+{1}{B} exiles any nonland permanent; covers artifact/enchantment hate lost with Vandalblast.
1x Aberrant Return → Requiting Hex (buy) — {B} instant, blight 1 = Auntie draw + Hapatra/Nest
   triggers, kills MV<=2 creature. Drops a 6-drop; recursion was over-represented.
1x Chimil, the Inner Sun → Fate Transfer (buy) — instant-speed counter MOVER: shift -1/-1
   counters from own creature (already drew) onto an opponent's (drain + kill + Patron/Hapatra/Nest).
   Chimil was a 6-mana off-theme value artifact.
1x Arcane Signet → Wall of Roots (buy) — once per turn, instant speed: -1/-1 counter for {G}
   = Auntie draw. Ramp count unchanged.
1x Midnight Banshee → Murderous Redcap (buy) — persist returns it with a -1/-1 counter (draw,
   snake, insect); ETB ping becomes counters under Everlasting Torment. Banshee (BBB 6-drop)
   shrank our own green creatures. No Solemnity/Vizier of Remedies in deck → no infinite.
1x Vandalblast → Chain Reaction — Vandalblast was never physically sleeved; Chain Reaction
   stays (owned). Damage sweeper that kills creatures with counters (Necroskitter/Reaper/
   Pillagers/Blowfly triggers) and becomes a -1/-1 counter wipe with Everlasting Torment.
   Vandalblast is now owned bench.
1x Burning Curiosity → Fain, the Broker (buy) — "{T}, Remove a counter from a creature you
   control: Treasure" works on -1/-1 counters, so spent blight counters become mana and
   creatures can be re-blighted for more Auntie draws. Also the deck's only repeatable sac
   outlet: sac creatures with -1/-1 counters for Blowfly/Scorpion God/Dread Tiller/Puca's/
   Shadow Urchin/Grave Venerations triggers; sac Murderous Redcap to persist it, then strip the
   counter for Treasure to reset persist. Untap costs {3}{B} → no infinite. Avoid putting his
   +1/+1 counters on own creatures holding -1/-1 counters (they annihilate).
