- Mountain → Sundering Eruption // Volcanic Fissure — flex land that doubles as {2}{R} LD spell
  (destroy a land + ramp a basic + non-fliers can't block); back is a red land. Land count stays 37.

- Two targeted LD pieces only — does NOT tip into mass land denial (Bracket 2 legal).
